# Explore links, backlinks and graphs

## Your goal

Navigate related notes and discover their connections.

## Before you start

Prepare at least two Markdown notes. The graph needs no semantic model.

## Steps

1. Type `[[`, enter a name and choose the target by its full path.
2. Open the target and inspect incoming backlinks.
3. Open the graph, search notes, filter folders and follow edges.
4. Use full relative paths for duplicate names. Prefer LeafLoom for moves and renames.

![Knowledge graph](../../../assets/screenshots/en/05-graph.png)

## What to expect

The graph represents local Markdown links, not semantic similarity or evidence reliability.

## If it does not work

For missing edges, check target spelling and filters. Recheck links after renaming externally.
