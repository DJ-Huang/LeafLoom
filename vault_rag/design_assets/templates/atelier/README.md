# ATELIER — Objects of light

Original editorial lighting concept, authored for LeafLoom on 2026-10-17. ATELIER and Sol are fictional concept names. No stock photographs, external fonts, remote resources or third-party brand assets are included.

## Editing

Three native artboards: editorial desktop landing, mobile landing and square product campaign. The amber glass lamp, tabletop, window light, shadows and material study are editable semantic layers composed from CSS gradients and shapes. All campaign copy is connected through stable `atelier.*` text bindings. English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese variants contain complete translations, source revisions and tailored headline sizing. Change shared Paper and ink styles for the brand palette. The collection button opens the square campaign.
